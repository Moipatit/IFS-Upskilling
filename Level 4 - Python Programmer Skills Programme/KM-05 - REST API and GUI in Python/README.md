
# Python Programmer Knowledge Module 5 Workbook

This guide explains how to access and use your **Learner Workbook**, which is essential for completing your formative and summative assessments.

---

## Accessing Your Workbook
- To begin this module, click on the [Python Programmer KM5 WB Link](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1MfSWNrbvVpx42flHaNaMp6_FGHBAB-VA3JUmrJt1ov4/copy) to make a copy of the workbook.  
Important: You will need to be signed into a Google account to access and copy the document. If you do not have a Gmail account, you may need to [create one](https://accounts.google.com/signup) to ensure access to the workbook. 
- When prompted, **make a copy of the document**. This copy is your personal workbook where you will complete all assessments.  
- Save your copy in a secure location (Google Drive or your computer) — you will need to access it frequently throughout the programme.

---

## Completing Assessments
- Each module contains **formative** and **summative** assessments.  
- These assessments are **autograded**. After clicking **Request Review** on your dashboard, you will automatically receive a **model answer via email**.  
- You may only click request review once you have completed **both formative and summative assessments** for a topic.  

---

## Portfolio of Evidence (PoE)
- Completing **all assessments** is crucial. They form part of your **Portfolio of Evidence (PoE)**, which demonstrates your competence in the programme.  
- If your workbook is incomplete, you will not meet the requirements to pass.  
- Each task builds your competency and demonstrates your learning progress. Skipping or leaving questions blank creates gaps in your PoE and negatively affects your results.  

---

## Note to the Learner
- You will demonstrate your competence through **continuous assessment**.  
- Formative assessments help you identify strengths and weaknesses. These can be completed in **groups, pairs, or individually**.  
- Summative assessments must always be completed **individually**.  
- All activities and assessments must be completed and submitted — they are evidence that you have successfully performed the required tasks.  

---

## Topic Naming Convention
Each topic has a unique code to help you locate and reference it easily:

- Example: **KM-01-KT01 – Problem solving skills for IT Professionals**  
  - **KM-01-KT01** → Topic code (acts like an ID number for organisation and facilitator reference).  
  - **Problem solving skills for IT Professionals** → Topic name (the lesson title).  

You’ll encounter other codes throughout the modules. Keep track of them — they help you stay organised and know exactly what you’re working on.

---

## Final Reminder
- Always make a copy of your workbook with your **Gmail account**.  
- Complete **all formative and summative assessments** — they are autograded and emailed back with model answers.  
- Ensure your workbook is fully completed, as it forms part of your **Portfolio of Evidence**.
