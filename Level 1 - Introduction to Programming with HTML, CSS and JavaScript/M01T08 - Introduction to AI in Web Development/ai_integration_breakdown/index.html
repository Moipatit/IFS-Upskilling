AI Integration Breakdown Of Netflix

Artificial Intelligence

Netflix's content strategy is significantly influenced by AI, which helps the company decide what new shows and movies to produce or acquire. AI-driven insights help Netflix make strategic decisions about content, leading to a more effective allocation of resources and a higher likelihood of producing successful shows and movies. This contributes to Netflix’s diverse and appealing content library.

Machine Learning

Netflix's recommendation engines using ML algorithms to suggest content that users are likely to enjoy. Machine learning models analyze vasts amount of data, including user behavior, such as what shows and movies are watched, search queries and even the time spent of different types of content. The models continuously learn and refine the recommendations based on this data.

This is done through the following:

  • Collaborative filtering: This involves analyzing user behaviour and preferences across the platform
  • Content-Based Filtering: Netflix examines the attributes of the content that users have previously watched, such as genre, director, and actors. It then suggests similar content based on these attributes.
  • Deep Learning Models: Advanced algorithms, including neural networks, analyze complex patterns in user data to predict what content users are likely to enjoy.

Generative AI

When browsing NetFlix, the user unknowingly places great importance on the thumbnail. The thumbnail alone is enough for many viewers to determine whether or not they should watch the video. Over time, Netflix realized that they cannot rely on titles alone, they had to also provide visually appealing thumbnails to entice their viewers. Netflix uses Generative AI to generate thumbnails by annotating and ranking hundreds of frames taken from a preexisting movie or TV program to determine which thumbnails are most likely to prompt a click from users.
